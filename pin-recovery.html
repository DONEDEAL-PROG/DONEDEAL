Recover your PIN

Customer support must approve your request after verifying your identity. Staff cannot view or choose your PIN.

1. Request recovery

Use this same browser and device where your details were saved.

Request PIN recovery

Opens customer support with a prefilled PIN-recovery message. Review it and send your ticket; staff will verify your identity before approving a reset.

2. Create your new PIN

After approval, support will supply a single-use recovery code through the verified contact channel. It expires after 15 minutes.

Recovery works only if an encrypted recovery backup was registered before the PIN was forgotten. It does not cancel orders or change payment status.
